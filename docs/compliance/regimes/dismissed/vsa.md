# VSA (Verschlusssachenanweisung) -- Compliance Assessment

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE
**Last reviewed:** 2026-04-06
**Next review:** 2027-04-06 (annual)

## Applicability Assessment

The VSA governs the handling of classified government information in Germany. BrickOS is a commercial health data platform with no government contracts, no access to classified material, and no involvement in government procurement processes. The platform handles personal health data under GDPR, not government-classified information under the VSA.

## Conditions for Re-evaluation

- BrickOS enters into contracts with German federal or state government agencies
- BrickOS begins processing data classified as VS-NfD or higher
- A government entity requests BrickOS to obtain facility security clearance
