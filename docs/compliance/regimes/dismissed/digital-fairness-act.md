# Digital Fairness Act -- Compliance Assessment

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE (not yet in force)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-04-06
**Next review:** 2027-04-06 (annual)

## Applicability Assessment

The Digital Fairness Act is a proposed EU regulation not yet enacted. It targets dark patterns, manipulative design, and unfair personalization in digital services. BrickOS has been pre-emptively evaluated and presents no concerns: pricing is transparent with no hidden fees, consent flows use clear opt-in language, there are no countdown timers or artificial urgency patterns, and the UI does not use manipulative design to influence user decisions.

## Conditions for Re-evaluation

- The Digital Fairness Act is formally adopted and enters into force
- Specific provisions create obligations beyond current GDPR consent and transparency requirements
- Delegated acts or guidance target health data platforms specifically
