# Digital Omnibus Directive -- Compliance Assessment

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE (no specific gaps)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-04-06
**Next review:** 2027-04-06 (annual)

## Applicability Assessment

The Digital Omnibus Directive strengthens consumer protection for digital services, covering transparency in pricing, cancellation rights, and data portability. While partially applicable to BrickOS as a digital service, no compliance gaps exist. BrickOS already provides clear tier-based pricing, straightforward account deletion with 30-day grace period, full data export (JSON + CSV), and no auto-renewal traps.

## Conditions for Re-evaluation

- Specific implementing measures are adopted that create new obligations for health platforms
- BrickOS introduces subscription models with complex pricing that could obscure total costs
- Consumer protection authorities issue guidance specifically targeting health data SaaS
