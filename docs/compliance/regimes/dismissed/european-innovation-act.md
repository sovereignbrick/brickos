# European Innovation Act -- Compliance Assessment

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE (opportunity, not obligation)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-04-06
**Next review:** 2027-04-06 (annual)

## Applicability Assessment

The European Innovation Act is not a compliance obligation but an enabling framework. It establishes regulatory sandboxes, innovation hubs, and funding mechanisms for digital innovation. BrickOS has no compliance requirements under this act. However, the platform's use of AI for health data interpretation could qualify for EU innovation sandbox participation under the AI Act's Article 53-54 sandbox provisions.

## Conditions for Re-evaluation

- EU innovation sandboxes for health AI become available in Germany
- BrickOS seeks regulatory sandbox participation for its AI health analysis features
- Funding programs under this framework become relevant for health data platforms
