# KRITIS (DE Critical Infrastructure) -- Compliance Assessment

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE
**Last reviewed:** 2026-04-06
**Next review:** 2027-04-06 (annual)

## Applicability Assessment

BrickOS is a pre-launch health data platform with no users in production yet. KRITIS designation for the health sector requires a provider to serve 500,000 or more users (BSI-KritisV threshold). BrickOS is orders of magnitude below this threshold and operates as a SaaS tool for individual health tracking, not as critical healthcare infrastructure.

## Conditions for Re-evaluation

- BrickOS exceeds 500,000 registered users in the health sector
- The BSI-KritisV thresholds for health-sector digital services are lowered
- BrickOS pivots to providing infrastructure for hospitals, insurers, or other KRITIS-designated entities
