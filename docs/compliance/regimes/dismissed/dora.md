# DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act) -- Compliance Assessment

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE
**Last reviewed:** 2026-04-06
**Next review:** 2027-04-06 (annual)

## Applicability Assessment

DORA (Regulation 2022/2554) targets financial entities -- banks, insurance companies, payment service providers, and their critical ICT third-party providers. BrickOS is a health data platform. While it accepts payments via Stripe and Bitcoin (Strike), it is not itself a financial entity, payment institution, or critical ICT provider to the financial sector.

## Conditions for Re-evaluation

- BrickOS becomes a licensed payment institution or e-money provider
- BrickOS is designated as a critical ICT third-party provider by an ESA (European Supervisory Authority)
- BrickOS begins providing services directly to banks, insurers, or regulated financial entities
