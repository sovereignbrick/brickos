A The entropy model

The one idea to convey: the participant supplies all randomness; the computer only reshapes it.

Your dice~100 rolls, the measurable backbone (2.585 bits each)
+
Optionalfresh photo & coin flips, independent insurance
→
SHA-256 whitenrepacks the pool into 256 uniform bits
→
+ checksum256 + 8 bits split into 24 words
  • No RNG. There is no Math.random / getRandomValues anywhere. Same dice in → same words out.
  • 256 bits is the ceiling. 100 fair rolls ≈ 258 bits (a small margin). The photo and coins cannot push past 256, they are insurance, not extra capacity.
  • Whitening can't create entropy. SHA-256 repacks whatever min-entropy you supplied into a uniform 256-bit string; it tolerates modest dice bias only while total min-entropy stays ≥ 256.
  • The passphrase is separate. Applied later via PBKDF2 to select a different wallet, the only element that adds strength beyond 256, because it is never stored with the words.

C Passphrase guidance

Strength = length from true randomness, not “complex-looking”. Use Diceware: 5 dice rolls pick one word from the 7776-word EFF list (≈12.9 bits/word). 30 rolls = 6 words ≈ 77 bits.

WordsEntropyVerdict
4~52 bitsminimum floor
6~77 bitsstrong (recommended)
7–8~90–103very strong

A single dictionary word, or a famous phrase, gives near-zero real entropy no matter how long.

Master seed vs. passphrase wallet, what to save
  • Always save the 24 words (steel, offline).
  • With a passphrase, also save it separately and exactly. Both are needed together.
  • The passphrase is not in the words and can't be recovered from them, lose it, lose the funds.
  • Don't save the 512-bit master seed; it's recomputed from words + passphrase.
Decoy pattern: the no-passphrase wallet is a plausible decoy (small, believable funds); real funds sit behind the passphrase, enabling deniability under coercion. Caveats: no typo protection and no recovery (a wrong character opens a different empty wallet), always test-receive a small amount to the passphrase wallet's first address before funding it.
