# Health Pillar (BrickOS)

*"Can I own and access my medical data?"*

The Health pillar covers sovereign medical data ownership -- your health records on your hardware, shareable on your terms.

## Apps

| App | Status | Description |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| [**Sovereign Health Intelligence**](sovereign-health/) | **Live** | Biomarker tracking, lab imports, AI health assistant, provider management |

## Cross-Pillar Integration

- **Finance:** Trade medical supplies on Sovereign Exchange
- **Attention:** Share health records with doctors via Sovereign Signal encrypted DMs
- **Energy:** Sovereign Almanac first aid guides link to health marker context
- **Technology:** Grab Bag includes encrypted health record export for forced migration
- **Community:** Emergency medication pooling coordinated via Sovereign Vote
