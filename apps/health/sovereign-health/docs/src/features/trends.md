# Trends & Analysis

Sovereign Health emphasizes trends over individual data points. A single measurement is a snapshot; a trend tells you whether you are improving, stable, or declining.

## Trend Charts

Every marker has a trend chart on its detail page. The chart displays:

- Individual data points plotted over time
- A connecting line showing the trajectory
- Color-coded background bands for reference ranges (green, yellow, red)
- Rolling average line for smoothing out daily variation

## Date Range Selection

Filter the trend view to focus on specific time periods:

- **7 days** for daily tracking markers (glucose, ketones, blood pressure)
- **30 days** for short-term trends
- **90 days** for quarterly reviews
- **1 year** for long-term patterns
- **All time** for the complete history

## Rolling Averages

The rolling average smooths out noise from day-to-day variation. Two options are available:

- **7-day rolling average** for markers measured daily or near-daily
- **30-day rolling average** for markers measured less frequently

The rolling average line helps you see the real direction of change, especially for markers like blood pressure or fasting glucose that naturally fluctuate.

## Protocol Filtering

If you have measurements taken under different protocols (standard eating, fasting 16:8, OMAD, extended fasting), you can filter the trend chart to show only one protocol at a time. This prevents misleading comparisons. A fasting glucose measurement taken during a 48-hour fast should not be plotted alongside a post-meal glucose reading without context.

## Reference Range Bands

The trend chart background is divided into colored bands:

- **Green band** covers the optimal range
- **Yellow band** covers the borderline range
- **Red area** covers values outside acceptable limits

When protocol filtering is active, the bands adjust to show protocol-specific reference ranges.

## Zone-Level Trends

The zone detail page shows mini trend charts for all markers in that zone, giving you a quick overview of which markers are improving and which need attention.

## Trend Direction Indicators

On the dashboard and zone pages, each marker shows a trend direction indicator:

- **Arrow up** with percentage change since the previous measurement
- **Arrow down** with percentage change
- **Horizontal line** when the value is stable (less than 2% change)

The arrow color reflects whether the direction is favorable. For markers where lower is better (glucose, LDL), a downward arrow is green. For markers where higher is better (HDL, vitamin D), an upward arrow is green.

## Phase Comparisons

Compare your averages across different time periods or protocol phases. This helps answer questions like: "How did my cholesterol panel change after 3 months of keto?" or "Are my inflammatory markers improving since I started supplementing with omega-3?"
