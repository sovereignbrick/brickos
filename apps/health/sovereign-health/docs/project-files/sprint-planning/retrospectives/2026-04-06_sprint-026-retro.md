# Sprint 026 Retrospective

**Sprint:** 026 - EU Compliance Frameworks & Launch Readiness
**Duration:** 2026-04-06 (1 day)
**Points planned:** 30
**Points delivered:** 30
**Version:** v0.36.0 -> v0.37.0

## What Went Well

- **Compliance framework structure** designed for auditors from the start: templates, evidence packages, traffic-light system, quarterly review cadence. 37 compliance documents created in one sprint.
- **AI Act classification** via voluntary conformity assessment was the right approach: do the full analysis, arrive at "limited risk" as a documented conclusion with risk score 2.2/5. Stronger than just claiming it.
- **Cookie audit** revealed no consent banner needed (all strictly necessary/functional). Saved development effort.
- **Newsletter consent** (#188) was already fully implemented from Sprint 009 - audit confirmed no gaps.
- **CycloneDX SBOMs** generated for both Rust and npm - CRA-compliant standard format.
- **here.now publication** of the compliance report provides a shareable URL for auditors/investors.
- **Parallel agent execution** (3 tracks) processed 14 regime assessments + 7 evidence packages + Art. 30 records simultaneously.

## What Didn't Go Well

- **Gamma document generation** hit plan limits (no header/footer on free plan, max 10 cards). Had to simplify. here.now HTML was the better approach - full control over branding and layout.
- **Cloudflare Access** blocked the ntfy tunnel unexpectedly - had to identify and remove the Access application. Should have checked Access policies before setting up the tunnel.

## Lessons Learned

1. **Do the conformity assessment, then conclude limited risk** - voluntary self-assessment is stronger than just claiming classification. An auditor can verify the methodology.
2. **Cookie audit before banner** - audit first, don't assume you need a consent banner. Many apps (especially sovereign/privacy-first ones) only use necessary cookies.
3. **Cloudflare Access and Tunnels interact** - Access policies apply to tunneled domains. Remove or bypass Access for services with their own auth.
4. **CycloneDX is the standard** - plain text SBOMs are useful for humans but CycloneDX JSON is what compliance tools expect.
5. **Compliance gaps split into milestones** - not everything can be fixed in one sprint. Pre-launch vs company-founding vs post-launch milestones keep priorities clear.
6. **here.now > Gamma** for custom branded reports - full HTML control, dark theme, no plan limitations.
