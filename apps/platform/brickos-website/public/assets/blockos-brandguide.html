01 Brand Foundation

What
BlockOS
stands for

BlockOS is a sovereign application platform — an operating layer for individuals and organisations who refuse to compromise on privacy, ownership, and digital autonomy. Built brick by brick, block by block, it assembles composable sovereign applications across the five dimensions of a free life: Money, Health, Technology, Data, and Attention.

The brand reflects this architecture: precise, austere, and purposefully constructed. Nothing decorative. Everything structural.

02 Logo & Mark

The
Mark

The BlockOS mark is a 3 × 2 isometric block grid seen from a fixed 26.565° viewpoint. Six blocks represent the six sovereign pillars. A checkerboard pattern of three filled and three outline blocks references the binary nature of sovereignty: owned versus available, built versus open. The platform slab beneath them is BlockOS itself — the foundation on which everything else rests.

Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to half the mark height on all four sides. The dashed boundary must remain free of all other graphic elements, text, and borders.

The minimum reproduction size for the mark alone is 16 × 16px. The full stacked lockup should never appear smaller than 120px wide.

03 Color

Black
& White
only

BlockOS uses no brand colour. The palette is a precise greyscale ramp — from absolute black to near-white — applied consistently across all surfaces. This is not minimalism as a trend: it is a deliberate statement that sovereignty needs no decoration. Colour is introduced exclusively through context (e.g. status indicators in product UI).

04 Typography

Two
Voices

BlockOS uses exactly two typefaces. Space Mono is the primary voice — technical, precise, monospaced. It appears in all UI labels, product copy, code, and the wordmark. Cormorant Garamond is the editorial voice — refined, literary, slow. It appears in large headlines, manifestos, and long-form content only. Never mix them in the same typographic role.

05 Isometric System

The
Grid
system

The mark is constructed on a precise isometric grid at a 26.565° projection angle (arctan 0.5 — the standard dimetric isometric). This angle produces tiles where the height is exactly half the width, making all proportions derivable from a single unit. The system is fully parametric: every dimension follows from the tile width TW.

Tile
Base rhombus. TH = TW ÷ 2. Projection angle 26.565°.
Block
Cube on tile. BH = TH (cube) or 1.25 × TH (tall). Three visible faces.
Platform
Slab beneath all blocks. PH = TH ÷ 4.7. Foundation = BlockOS.

06 Usage Rules

Do &
Don't

The mark's power comes from its consistency. Any distortion, recolouring, or unauthorised modification undermines the precision that the brand communicates. When in doubt: do less.

✓ Do
  • Use the mark on pure black, near-black, or pure white / warm-white backgrounds only
  • Maintain minimum clear space on all sides (½ mark height)
  • Scale uniformly — the mark is always square-proportioned
  • Use Space Mono 700 for the wordmark — no substitutions
  • Use the provided dark or light variant as supplied — do not create intermediate versions
  • Allow the slogan lines to span the full wordmark width via textLength
  • Reproduce the mark as SVG wherever possible for crispness
✗ Don't
  • Do not recolour the mark — no tints, no colour fills, no gradients
  • Do not rotate or skew the isometric mark
  • Do not place the mark on busy photographs or patterned backgrounds
  • Do not stretch or distort the wordmark — use lengthAdjust="spacing" only
  • Do not add drop shadows, glows, or embossing to the mark
  • Do not use Cormorant Garamond for the wordmark or slogan
  • Do not reproduce the mark below 16px or the full lockup below 120px wide
  • Do not add decorative punctuation to the slogan
Digital Usage
Web
Always deliver the mark as inline SVG or .svg file. PNG only as fallback. Minimum: 2× resolution for retina screens.
Favicon
Use the mark-only variant at 16 × 16, 32 × 32, and 64 × 64px. On dark browser chrome, the dark variant. Use a #070707 background tile.
Social / OG
Open Graph image: 1200 × 630px, dark background, centred full lockup at 40% canvas width. Export as PNG 1× from the brand SVG.
